Personal Decision & Problem Solver

Decide better, every day.

One app for your decisions, tasks, money, habits and study — built by a three-person team in C++, with a web dashboard that reads and writes the exact same data files as the console app. No fake data, no demo numbers: what you see is what you saved.

Sign in with Google, or register here with a username and password — no invitation needed. Your tasks, budget, habits and history are private to your account: one user cannot see another user's data. First registered user becomes ADMIN.

🧠 Decide

Daily decisions, priority scores, task prioritization, compare options, multi-task planning, deadline alerts and plain-language explanations.

💰 Manage money

Budget, income and expense, savings goals, weekly and monthly reports, and TXT/CSV export of your real transactions.

📖 Build habits

Habit tracking with a true consecutive-day streak, study planner, time allocation, and a daily summary report.

How it works

  1. Register — the first account is ADMIN, later accounts are USER.
  2. Log in — you get a session token; it is never your password, and it is never written to a file.
  3. Use any section — every button calls a real C++ API endpoint, and every change is saved to a plain-text file the console app can also read.
  4. Back up — Overview has Backup and Restore for your decision records.

Team

Rifat — system core, authentication, profile, admin, password, time allocation, study planner, dashboard and C++ backend. Nuriya — daily decision, priority, tasks, compare, status, deadlines, explanations, multi-task. Sweety — budget, income/expense, savings, habits, streak, reports, export.

Design note: the light theme is inspired by the Panze habit-tracker mobile design (deep navy, pink-to-blue gradient, large rounded cards and pill buttons) — no content, text or images were copied from any reference site. A dark theme is still available from the theme toggle.

AI Daily Decision

Ask the AI for a recommendation on a decision, with optional options to compare. The AI analyses your input and returns one clear recommendation with reasons. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. Input is limited to 4000 bytes (a Bengali character counts as 3 bytes) and each option to 500 bytes.

AI Task Analyzer

Ask the AI to analyse a task: priority, urgency and concrete next steps. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. Description is limited to 4000 bytes (a Bengali character counts as 3 bytes), title to 500 bytes, and deadline (free text, not a date) to 100 bytes.

AI Study Planner

Ask the AI to build a study plan for your subjects: a realistic daily schedule, subject priorities and a revision strategy. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. Each subject is limited to 200 bytes (a Bengali character counts as 3 bytes), hours per day to 0.5–24, and the optional description to 4000 bytes. The exam date must be today or in the future.

AI Problem Solver

Ask the AI to analyse a problem and suggest solutions: problem analysis, possible solutions, a recommended solution and concrete action steps. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. The problem is limited to 4000 characters (counted as characters, not bytes — a Bengali character counts as one).

AI Budget Assistant

Ask the AI to review your income, expenses and savings goal: a budget plan, spending recommendations and savings progress. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. The AI never calculates — the server computes all figures (totals, remaining, deficit, savings rate, goal progress) first and the AI only advises on them. Income is optional and 0 counts as provided; expenses are up to 20 rows; a savings goal needs all three fields (name, target, saved).

AI Habit Assistant

Ask the AI to review one of your habits: your real streak (daily or weekly), completion rate and a plan to keep it going. Your input is sent to the AI as data only — never as an instruction. The server computes your streak from your own habit log first — the AI only advises on it, and your habit log is never changed. The habit name is limited to 100 characters; the optional target streak is 1–365; the optional reference date must be within 2 days of today (leave it empty to use the server's date).

AI History

Your past AI answers, newest first — only your own entries are shown, never another user's. Filter by feature, or delete an entry you no longer need.
